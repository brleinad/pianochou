# Bundled fonts

Both fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; license texts are next to this file.

- **Bravura** (`Pianochou/Fonts/Bravura.otf`): SMuFL music font by Steinberg.
  Source: https://github.com/steinbergmedia/bravura (redist/otf).
- **Hanken Grotesk** (`Pianochou/Fonts/HankenGrotesk.ttf`): variable weight font.
  Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/hankengrotesk
