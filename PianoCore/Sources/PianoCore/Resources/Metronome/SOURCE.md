Metronome sounds, trimmed, faded out, made mono and normalized:

- `accent.wav` (first beat of the bar): "Metronom Claves.wav"
- `beat.wav` (other beats): "Metronom Taktell Junior.wav"

By Christoph Eckert, released into the public domain ("the files are hereby in public domain"),
from metro_christeck.zip at https://rncbc.org/drupal/node/566 (Qtractor metronome sounds).
